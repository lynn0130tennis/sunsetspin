About Sunset Spin Invitational

Sunset Spin Invitational is an organization dedicated to promoting excellence in competitive tennis. We host tournaments and events that bring together players of all skill levels to compete, network, and celebrate the sport we love.

Our vision is to build a vibrant tennis community where every player feels connected, inspired, and motivated to grow — creating meaningful relationships and unforgettable experiences through the game of tennis.

Registration Information

📋 What to Include

  • Full name & contact info
  • USTA rating (if applicable)
  • Tournament selection
  • Division preference
  • Partner info (for doubles)

⏰ Important Dates

  • Early registration: 2 weeks prior
  • Standard registration: 1 week prior
  • Late registration: 3 days prior
  • Deadline: 24 hours before event

💰 Fees & Refunds

  • Entry fees vary by tournament
  • Full refund up to 7 days prior
  • 50% refund up to 3 days prior
  • No refunds within 48 hours
